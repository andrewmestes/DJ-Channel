Having spent over 15 years honing his craft, Kyle Channell, who goes by the stage name “DJ CHANNELL,” has established himself as a full-time DJ. He is renowned for his versatility as an open-format DJ and is celebrated for delivering electrifying performances that cater to diverse audiences.

DJ CHANNELL has a remarkable talent for seamlessly blending different genres of music, which has earned him high-profile collaborations with prominent industry brands such as Global Dance Festival at Red Rocks, Red Bull performing at X-Games, GoPro Mountain Games, US Burton Open, and Pikes Peak International Fan Fest, among others. Regardless of the size of the venue, DJ CHANNELL uses his expertise to blend various genres and create an unforgettable experience for his audiences.
